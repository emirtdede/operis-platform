# Matching, Payment and Contract Disclaimer

**Last Updated:** 2026-09-09  
**Version:** v1.0  
**Legal Nature:** Definitive Liability Disclaimer, Lawsuit Immunity, and Operational Disclosure

## 1. Core Principle, Non-Profit Foundation, and Neutral Intermediary Role

The Platform operates as a non-profit, free, open, and independent listing and communication environment bringing together technology, software, and design professionals with project creators.

The Platform:
1. **Does Not Receive or Hold Funds:** No financial transactions or disbursements take place through the platform. The platform does not provide escrow, wallet, payment guarantees, deposit holding, or credit insurance; it is not a payment or credit institution.
2. **Is Not Party to Service Contracts:** Offer acceptance opens a private bilateral match and direct communication channel. It does not constitute a platform-guaranteed, endorsed, or co-signed final service contract. Bilateral legal relations exist exclusively between counterparties.
3. **Does Not Adjudicate Commercial Disputes:** Disputes regarding deliverables, milestones, quality, fraud, or payments must be settled directly between counterparties or before competent judicial authorities; the platform does not act as an arbitrator, mediator, or tribunal.
4. **Zero Commercial Risk:** The Platform has zero financial interest and assumes zero commercial or operational risk in bilateral transactions.
5. **Lawsuit and Aggrieved Party Immunity:** Users—even in the event of default, scam, non-payment, or deficient deliverable by a counterparty—must direct all claims solely against the offending counterparty. Users agree not to file lawsuits or damage claims against the Platform, its directors, or employees, and irrevocably discharge all such rights.

Users are individually liable for tax filings, corporate declarations, invoicing compliance, and statutory duties. No civil, administrative, or tax penalties may be attributed or passed through to the Platform.

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, users acknowledge and accept these limitations upon using the platform.
